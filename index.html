ABOUT THE PROJECT

About LAN Watch

LAN Watch is a web-based version of Group 3's Python-Based LAN Network Traffic Analyzer and Protocol Monitor. It is an educational tool for observing permitted traffic on a selected local network interface and making packet information easier to understand.

What it does

It displays available packet details such as source and destination addresses, protocol, size, and capture time. Live summaries, device views, gateway-health checks, and downloadable reports help users explore how common network protocols work.

QUICK START

How to use LAN Watch

  1. Sign in

    Open the main page and sign in, or create an account and complete email verification if asked.

  2. Choose an interface

    Select the network adapter connected to the LAN you are authorized to monitor. Use Refresh if the adapter list needs updating.

  3. Choose device discovery

    The ping-at-start option is checked by default. It checks hosts once on the selected private IPv4 subnet (up to 256 addresses); devices that block ping may not reply.

  4. Start the capture

    Press Start and allow a little time for packets to arrive. “No activity” means the capture has not received packets yet.

  5. Explore the traffic

    Review the client list or network tree, traffic charts, health status, and “What to do next” advice. Use packet filters to narrow results; double-click a packet row for details.

  6. Stop and export

    Press Stop when finished. Use the export buttons for filtered packets, selected rows, or a network report in CSV or PDF. Exports are password-protected; enter and confirm a password of at least 8 characters. Your browser controls the download folder.

  7. Open a saved capture

    Press Packets… on the main page to open a saved capture from the list or choose a .msgpack file from your device. Each capture run is stored by the local server in session_packets.

Permissions: Packet capture may require administrator privileges and a compatible capture driver. Only monitor a private network you own or have permission to inspect.

HELP & GUIDANCE

Frequently asked questions

Will LAN Watch show every packet and device on my network?

No. It can only display packets visible to the selected network interface. On switched Wi-Fi or Ethernet networks, traffic exchanged directly between other devices may not be visible.

How does the device list find hosts?

It learns IPv4 devices from captured traffic and can optionally ping hosts once when capture starts. The ping check is limited to the selected private subnet and up to 256 addresses. Devices that block ping may not appear.

Does LAN Watch decrypt HTTPS or show private message contents?

No. The analyzer presents packet metadata and available protocol/header details. It does not decrypt HTTPS traffic.

Why might capture require administrator permissions?

Packet-capture drivers and operating systems may require elevated permissions. A compatible capture backend must also be installed and available.

What do the network health and congestion messages mean?

Health checks report the observed response from the default gateway. A congestion warning is based on gateway response and packet loss only; LAN Watch cannot identify which device, if any, is responsible.

Where do exported files go?

Files are downloaded by your browser. They usually appear in its Downloads folder, unless your browser is configured to ask where to save each file or use another folder.

What if I forget the export password?

Exports are password-protected. Keep the password you entered when exporting; LAN Watch cannot recover it for you.
